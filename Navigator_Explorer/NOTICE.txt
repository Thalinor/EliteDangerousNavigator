(N) Explorer - what was adapted, and from whom

Pioneer (System Value) plugin for EDMC
  https://github.com/Silarn/EDMC-Pioneer
  Copyright 2024 Jeremy Rimpo. GNU General Public License, version 2 or (at your option) any later version.
  Pioneer is itself inspired by Economical Cartographics (Nils Steinger) and uses value formulae from MattG.

What we took from it, and where it lives here:
  labels.py   the star description and planet class letters (adapted from src/pioneer/util.py; emoji removed,
              plain-text tags used instead).
  model.py    the rules for what counts toward the system value: the honk share paid with the main star, the Fully
              Scanned and Fully Mapped bonuses, hiding mapped bodies from the valuable list, the B# / NB# counts.
  values.py   written from MattG's published formula and constants; its results are checked against Pioneer's.
  The layout idea (valuable bodies, biological signals, per-body values, totals) follows Pioneer's panel.

Everything else (the surface map, sample rings, the panel and its theme handling) is new code for this suite.
Because Pioneer is "version 2 or later", these parts are used here under version 3 or later, as the rest of the suite is.
Ideas for the surface map were also inspired by EDRhinoSpotter (Fumlop, GPL-3.0); no code from it is used.

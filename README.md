# Navigator Suite - downloads taken down for now

Hi everyone,

Due to unforeseen circumstances, we've decided to fork and rebuild EDMC as a new app called
**Elite Navigator**. It's in development right now, and all of the Navigator plugins will work
with Elite Navigator.

Because of that, the plugin downloads that used to be here have been taken down for the time
being. The repository itself is staying open.

The reasons will be shared later. It isn't something I'm going to discuss publicly right now,
but I will make a fuller post soon.

I want to be clear about one thing: I love, support and appreciate Elite Dangerous Market
Connector and the people who make it. At this time, this was simply a decision that had to be
made.

Elite Navigator should be available for beta testing in the next couple of weeks. Sorry for the
inconvenience, and thank you for your patience.

o7 Commander Thalinor
